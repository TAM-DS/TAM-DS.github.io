Gradio · Supply chain & cybersecurity

Business risk crosses departments.

The workflow should connect the pieces: supplier resilience, energy exposure, semiconductor dependencies, and security operations.

I use Gradio to turn Python workflows into interactive interfaces business reviewers can explore. Its team reported more than one million monthly developers in April 2025; integration with Hugging Face Spaces supports rapid demonstration and sharing.

Three lines create the interface. The engineering behind it makes the workflow worth using.

import gradio as gr
demo = gr.Interface(fn=analyze_risk, inputs="text", outputs="text")
demo.launch()

Illustrative example: analyze_risk is an existing Python function. The actual dashboards use richer layouts and explicit workflow controls.
